03

Months later, search "boots." Kennery tells you where you put it.

A home inventory you'll actually keep up

Most home inventory apps want you to catalog everything you own — categories, values, barcodes, receipts — and then charge a subscription to hold your own list. That's a project, and projects get abandoned in week two.

Kennery is one running list you add to in ten seconds whenever you put something somewhere non-obvious — so future you doesn't have to open six closets to find the spare charger. Same job, none of the homework.

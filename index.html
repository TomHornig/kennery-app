Kennery is a small, private index for the things you've tucked away. Write down where it is, once — search for it months later, and find it in seconds.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone

How it works

01

Add an item — a short note on where it lives. "Winter boots, under the bed, blue bin."

02

Snap a photo if it helps you recognize it faster. Totally optional.

03

Months later, search "boots." Kennery tells you where you put it.

What it isn't

It's not a home-inventory system for insurance, not a moving-box labeler, not a warehouse tool repurposed for home use. It's one running list you add to in ten seconds whenever you put something somewhere non-obvious — so future you doesn't have to open six closets to find the spare charger.

Privacy

No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no ads. Everything you add — names, locations, photos — stays on your device. You can export a personal backup any time, entirely under your control.
